    YOUR EVERYDAY MONEY BOOK

    Less to remember.
    More room to trade.

    Speak a sale. Keep a record. Know how your business is doing.

    • Speak naturally. Use the languages supported by your book, including mixed-language speech.
    • Keep the details together. Record sales, expenses and credit without stopping to fill out a form.
    • See the bigger picture. Check your daily totals and create a trading statement.

    Choose speech and reply languages at the top of your book. English replies are the default; Pidgin replies are available for the Nigerian pack. Shona / English uses USD and English replies. Finish any pending question before switching.

    ONE ENTRY AT A TIME

    Say it.
    Check it. Keep it.

    • 01 · Hold the microphone. Say the item, quantity and amount. Release when you finish.
    • 02 · Check the readback. Listen to the amount and read the reply. If something is wrong, say “no” and give the correction.
    • 03 · Answer any question. If an amount is unclear, Sauti asks instead of guessing. You can always type your answer.

    Try an example — you can edit it before sending

    YOUR CHOICE, ALWAYS

    Know what you share.

    • See every recorded transmission. The byte counter stays visible above your book. Open Privacy to see its details.
    • Understand voice processing. Online speech recognition sends your audio to the speech service. Online readback also sends the reply text.
    • Choose whether this app keeps clips. Retention is off by default. You can use your book without enabling it; turning it off stops future retention.
